# Claims and non-claims

## Supported by the frozen public analysis

The frozen B50 analysis supports a conditional statement that physical adjacency in
the tested prime-gap transition-operator representation is associated with
successor-geometry specificity relative to the frozen matched controls.

The effect was reproduced across multiple same-corpus phase offsets. The mechanism
audit identified a minimal two-channel core, based on turn-change and
density-scale-change, that recaptures the principal full-score effect under the
frozen B50.11/B50.12 protocol.

## Important limitations

The repository does not contain a successful disjoint arithmetic-interval
replication. B50.9 is retained specifically to document that limitation.

The observed statistics are conditional on the frozen representation, windowing,
matching, and score construction. They should not be interpreted as a universal
law without additional independent validation.

## Explicit non-claims

This repository does not claim:

- prediction of individual prime numbers;
- a generative Markov law for the primes;
- a proof of a new prime-number theorem;
- independent-corpus replication where none was completed;
- a proof or disproof of the Riemann Hypothesis;
- a Hilbert–Pólya realization;
- that exploratory representation choices are uniquely canonical.

These boundaries are part of the scientific result, not editorial caveats.
