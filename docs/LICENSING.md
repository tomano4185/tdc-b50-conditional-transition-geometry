# Licensing

The public repository deliberately separates software licensing from licensing
of research/publication materials.

## Code

Executable source code and scripts are licensed under the MIT License.

SPDX identifier: `MIT`

## Research and publication materials

Non-code public research materials are licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International.

SPDX identifier: `CC-BY-4.0`

This includes frozen derived outputs, tables, figures, documentation, manifests,
bibliographic metadata, and citation metadata unless a file explicitly states
otherwise.

## Frozen artifacts

Applying CC BY 4.0 to the distributed frozen outputs does not authorize editing
them in place while claiming byte-level reproduction. Reusers may create adapted
materials under the license, but the repository's reproducibility claims continue
to refer only to the SHA-256-bound frozen bytes.

## Third-party rights

References, titles, factual bibliographic information, and any third-party
material remain subject to applicable rights. This repository does not claim
ownership of third-party works merely because they are cited or described.
